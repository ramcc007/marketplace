Video interviewing software lets a hiring team run interviews on video without arranging each one by hand. Some tools record answers that candidates give on their own time. Others run live calls. Good tools also book the interview and give reviewers a common way to rate what they see. This page compares six tools that meet all three inclusion criteria on the vendor's own pages. The category needs eight qualifying tools, so the list is two short.

## What a qualifying tool must show

A tool qualifies when its vendor pages show three things. It must offer one-way or live video interviews, and either one is enough. It must offer interview scheduling, meaning candidates or recruiters can book an interview through the product. It must offer reviewer scoring, meaning reviewers rate interviews on a scale or scorecard inside the product.

A tool that records answers but has no scheduling does not qualify. Neither does a tool that schedules interviews but gives reviewers no way to score them. A claim that a feature exists in a partner app does not count unless the vendor's own pages state it.

## The six tools

Jobma, Spark Hire, Vidcruiter and HireVue each have a product profile from the first draft. Bullhorn and Xobin were added on the second pass. Each profile records the vendor page behind every claim.

Jobma's homepage describes structured, on-demand video interviews and live interviews with hiring managers and panelists. It says candidates can self-book from calendars, and that reviewers rate against the same scorecard. Its pricing page names no plans or prices.

Spark Hire's pricing page lists applicant tracking plans and a Video Interviews plan. Recruit Pro is listed from $335 a month and Recruit Growth from $499 a month, both on annual billing. The Video Interviews plan is listed from $249 a month on annual billing, or from $299 a month billed monthly.

Vidcruiter's homepage describes answers that candidates record on their own schedule, live video sessions with rating guides, and self-booking from synced interviewer calendars. Its pricing page asks visitors to fill in a form and names no plans.

HireVue's product page describes on-demand interviews, live interviews with the interviewer on the call, automated scheduling and shared scorecards. Its pricing page lists products but no pricing tiers, and it offers a demo request.

Bullhorn's page lists on-demand and live video interviewing. It says candidates can schedule their own interview from any device, and that clients can rate and score candidates in real time. The page shows no prices and offers a live demo and a partner demo.

Xobin's video interview page describes one-way video assessments with structured scoring. Its live interview documentation says candidates can book slots against real-time interviewer availability, and that scorecards are filled out during the session. The pages read show no price.

## Prices are hard to compare

Only Spark Hire publishes figures among the six. The other five quote a price, or show none in the text read, so their cost cannot be set beside Spark Hire's from the pages alone. The Spark Hire figures exclude sales tax and vary by company size, according to its pricing page. The page shows dollar amounts without naming the currency in the text read, so those figures are recorded as shown.

Jobma, Vidcruiter, HireVue, Bullhorn and Xobin are recorded as quote only. Their profiles carry one plan with no amount, and a note that the vendor publishes no price on the pages read.

## Scheduling and live interviews

Scheduling is where a tool saves a recruiter the most time. Jobma and Vidcruiter describe calendar sync, so candidates can pick a slot without an email exchange. Bullhorn says candidates can schedule from a phone, laptop or any browser, and that its scheduling syncs client calendars for individual, back-to-back and panel interviews. Xobin says recruiters can coordinate times by hand or let candidates book slots against interviewer availability.

Spark Hire lists self-service scheduling on its applicant tracking plans, and automated panel scheduling on the higher tiers. HireVue lists automated scheduling without describing how it works. Only the Spark Hire and Bullhorn pages name panel scheduling. Ask the other vendors how they book a panel of several interviewers before you choose.

## Reviewer scoring

Each tool scores in its own way. Vidcruiter describes structured scorecards with adjustable rating scales, and human reviewers who score and comment. HireVue describes standard rating scales and shared scorecards. Jobma says reviewers rate against the same scorecard and leave comments on specific answers. Spark Hire lists configurable scorecards on its Video Interviews plan. Bullhorn says reviewers can rate and score candidates in real time, and Xobin fills its scorecards in during the live session.

Automated scores and human ratings answer different questions. Vidcruiter describes automated scoring that evaluates each response against role-specific rubrics, so ask each vendor to explain how its automated score is produced, in writing, before a reviewer relies on it.

## Tools that did not qualify

Several tools were checked and left out. Willo lists one-way video interviews and custom scorecards. Its pages do not state a way to book interviews in the product, and its comparison pages disagree about live booking. Hireflix is built around one-way interviews that candidates record on their own time. Its pages present that as removing the need to schedule, and its scorecards come through an integration, not the product itself.

Zoho Recruit's pages state one-way and live video interviews, recruiter scheduling from candidate profiles, and star or thumb ratings in its evaluation forms. It is not counted here because its pricing page could not be read reliably. It can be added once its pricing is confirmed on the vendor page.

Jobvite and iCIMS state video interviewing and scheduling, but the product pages read do not state reviewer scoring of interviews. Talview describes live interview rooms, but its homepage does not state scheduling or reviewer scoring. Interviewstream lists one-way and live formats, but its pages do not state scheduling or scoring. Harver mentions video and scheduling in general terms and does not state the format or a scorecard.

Manatal and Recruit CRM describe no video interview format that the text read could confirm. Recruitee refers to video conferencing through third-party tools rather than a native feature. TalentLyft states self-scheduling and scorecards for its recruiting product, but does not state a video interview format. SmartRecruiters' homepage makes no video interview claim. The Workable page we tried returned not found. One Way Interview screens without scheduling by design. The VireUp page returned no text.

## Before you sign

Record a test interview with a colleague in each tool on your shortlist. Check how long the recordings are kept and who can open them. Ask for the currency and minimum term on any quote, and confirm in writing whether scheduling and scoring are included in the price. A homepage can list a feature that the plan you buy does not include, so check the feature on a real interview.

Five of the six tools on this page publish no price in the text read, so a written quote is the only way to compare their cost with Spark Hire's published figures. Ask each one for the currency, the unit and the minimum term.
